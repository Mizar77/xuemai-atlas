# ACL 2024–2026 award-author audit

Primary award sources:

- https://2024.aclweb.org/program/best_papers/
- https://2025.aclweb.org/program/awards/
- https://2026.aclweb.org/program/best_papers/

Scope: Best Paper, Best Theme, Best Resource, Best Social Impact and Outstanding Paper. The atlas adds only current faculty or independent PIs in its six supported regions. Student authors, ordinary industry contributors and authors based outside the current six-region taxonomy are not converted into faculty nodes.

## Explicit exclusions / follow-up boundary

- Non-faculty authors and industry-only researchers are retained only in the conference author list.
- Faculty in Japan, South Korea, Australia, Israel, India, Africa and other regions are recorded as out of the current geographic taxonomy, not incorrectly assigned to Europe/US/Asia.
- Some outstanding-paper author lists contain many collaborators. A person is added only after a current faculty or independent-PI page is found.
